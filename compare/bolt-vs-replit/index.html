Bolt vs Replit: which one to pick

We don't name one overall winner: the right pick depends on what you are building. This is how the two compare in each use case where at least one of them is on the podium.

Use caseBoltReplit

What we say about Bolt

  • My first web app, with no coding experience (#2): The most generous free plan for trying before you pay, and you choose the framework.
  • A SaaS MVP with login, a database and payments (#3): Fast for the complete prototype; it charges per member, so it gets expensive for a team.
  • A polished interface or landing page, fast (#3): Gets the job done, with less polished interfaces than v0.
  • A mobile app (#1): Builds apps with Expo, the shortest path from a prompt to iOS and Android.

What we say about Replit

  • My first web app, with no coding experience (#3): The most complete and the lowest entry price, but you see the code from minute one: more power, steeper curve.
  • A SaaS MVP with login, a database and payments (#2): Agent, editor, terminal and deployment in one place. Better when your backend has its own logic.
  • A mobile app (#3): A solid second choice if you prefer to control the code.
  • An internal tool or a data dashboard (#1): Hybrid editor and terminal: easier to adapt and to connect to your data.
