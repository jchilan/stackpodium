Cursor vs Devin Desktop: the reference editor, or the former Windsurf

Pick Cursor if you want the reference AI editor and you will watch the usage pool, because third-party models are billed separately from Premium up. Pick Devin Desktop if you want the former Windsurf: Devin Desktop Pro is listed at $20/mo.

Questions

How do they differ?

Devin Desktop is the former Windsurf and takes the same editor approach as Cursor. Its listed Pro price is $20/mo. Cursor is the reference editor, built on VS Code, and its cheapest paid plan is Plus at $8/mo.

What do they cost?

Cursor's individual plans are Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, and Ultra $200 a month. The pricing page does not list a $20 Pro plan. Devin Desktop Pro is $20/mo. Older Windsurf Pro subscriptions at $15 were grandfathered.

Which bill is easier to predict?

Cursor's lowest paid plan is Plus at $8/mo, and the pricing page says that plan includes Grok models only. Devin Desktop Pro is $20/mo, with frontier models inside the included quota. From Cursor Premium up, third-party models are billed separately, so the extra spend can surprise you. Neither sticker replaces the current price on the official page.

Are these prices current?

They were checked in November 2026. Prices change often. Always check the official page before you pay.
