Sponsor StackPodium

StackPodium is read by people who are about to pick a tool: each page answers one question, “what should I build this with?”. A sponsor reaches them at that moment.

What you can sponsor

  • The weekly email. One sponsor per issue, at the top, marked as sponsored.
  • A use-case page. One sponsored card next to the podium, marked as sponsored and visually separate from the ranking.
  • A launch note. Your release in “What changed”, marked as sponsored.

Our rules

  • Sponsorship never moves a tool up or down a podium. Rankings follow the published scoring method.
  • Every paid placement is labelled.
  • We only accept tools that fit builders, and we may decline.
  • You get a report with the real numbers: sends, opens and clicks. We do not estimate or inflate.
