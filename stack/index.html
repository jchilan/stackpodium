FAQ

What do I get for $19?

One Stack Report, by email, for the project you describe: three ranked tools, the monthly cost of each, the reason for each pick, and first steps with prompts. You do not get a built app.

When does it arrive?

Within 24 hours of PayPal confirming the payment. If a day passes and nothing is in your inbox, check spam, then write to sponsors@stackpodium.com.

Do tools pay to be recommended?

No. The report follows the same scoring as the free podiums. If a recommended tool has an affiliate link, the report says so, and it never changes the order.

What fits?

One project you could start this month: a web app, a mobile app, an automation, an agent, an internal tool, a small game. If it's a large product, describe the first version.

What happens to my description?

The form posts it to PayPal with the payment, in the custom field (256 characters). We use it to write your report and we don't publish it.
