What's in the pack

One email, three parts, written for the idea you sent. The aim is a small thing you can play, not a finished commercial game.

  1. 01

    Game design one-pager

    The player, the core action, how a round starts and ends, and what the prototype leaves out on purpose.

  2. 02

    Build plan to a prototype

    Ordered steps from an empty folder to something you can open in a browser and play. Each step says what "done" looks like.

  3. 03

    Ready-to-paste prompts

    Prompts written for Cursor or a similar AI coding tool, in the same order as the plan. You paste them. You don't have to invent them.

Two levels, one price

Pick the reader, not a bigger game. Both packs cover the same idea. The voice and the amount of explanation change.

Level A

Beginner (never coded)

For someone who has not written code. Every step says what to click, what the words mean, and how to tell that it worked.

  • Terms are explained the first time they show up.
  • Steps are short, and each one ends with a check.
  • Prompts ask the AI to explain the files it creates.
Level B

Vibe coder (uses AI coding tools)

For someone who already drives Cursor or a similar tool. The plan stays lean. Most of the page is prompts, in order.

  • No tour of the editor.
  • Decisions and scope up front, then the prompt sequence.
  • Prompts assume you can run the project and read a diff.

FAQ

What do I get for $9?

One Plan Pack, by email, for the idea you submit: a design one-pager, a step-by-step plan to a playable prototype, and prompts for Cursor or a similar AI coding tool. You do not get a finished game, art production, or a store listing.

When does it arrive?

Within 24 hours of PayPal confirming the payment. If a day passes and nothing is in your inbox, check spam, then write to sponsors@stackpodium.com.

What is the difference between the two levels?

Beginner (never coded) explains every step. Vibe coder (uses AI coding tools) is a shorter, prompt-heavy plan for people who already work that way. Same idea, same $9, different amount of explanation.

Do I need to know how to code?

No, if you pick Beginner. You still do the building, with the plan and the prompts. PlayForge does not log into your computer. The coming-soon Premium is the version where we build the prototype for you.

Which email do you send it to?

If you fill in "Delivery email", we send the pack there. Leave it blank and we use the email on the PayPal account that pays. Use the extra field when those two addresses differ.

What happens to my game idea?

The form posts it to PayPal with the payment, in the custom field (256 characters). We use it to write your pack. We don't publish it. The StackPodium privacy page covers the rest of this website. This order is an extra step that page doesn't describe: there is no account here, and PayPal processes the payment.

Is the $29 Premium available?

Not yet. Premium means we build the playable browser prototype and email you a link. The card below is a teaser. The only thing you can buy today is the $9 Plan Pack.

What kind of idea fits?

Something that can become a small browser prototype: one screen, one main action, a clear way to win or lose. A commercial game with a campaign, accounts, and a content pipeline does not fit a $9 plan. Describe that smaller slice.
