Weekend build plan

Seven steps, in order. Each one names what "done" looks like. You need a computer, Cursor or a similar AI coding tool, and a current Node.js so Vite can run. There is no account on this site.

  1. 01

    Scaffold the project

    In an empty folder, create a Vite + TypeScript app and add Phaser 3. Boot a 960x640 scene named BankScene, with arcade physics on. Title it "Sixty-Second Job". Subtitle: "60 seconds. Fill the bag. Get to the car." No assets yet.

    Done when: npm run dev shows that title and subtitle, and a TypeScript check reports no errors.

  2. 02

    Block in the bank

    One screen. Outer walls. The lobby fills the lower part, an open vault sits top-left, the front door is a gap at the bottom, the side door is a gap on the right, and a back door opens into a small alley at the top-right. Put a car tile in the alley and do not make it solid. Add two counter blocks and a vault cash marker. Labels are enough.

    Done when: you can see the lobby, the vault, all three doors, and the car, and a body cannot pass through a wall.

  3. 03

    Move the thief

    A small rectangle is you. WASD and the arrow keys both move it. Cap the speed, and normalize diagonal movement so the slant is not a shortcut. Collide with walls. Keep the camera fixed.

    Done when: you can walk the lobby and the vault, stop at every wall, stand on the car tile, and a diagonal is not faster than a straight line.

  4. 04

    Fill the bag

    Three piles, taken once: $2,000, $2,000, and $3,000 in the vault. Overlap adds that amount to a bag number on screen and removes the pile.

    Done when: taking every pile shows $7,000, and walking back over an empty spot adds nothing.

  5. 05

    Run the clock

    Show the remaining seconds. Leave the clock at 60 until the first movement, then subtract one second at a time, and stop at 0.

    Done when: standing still at the start leaves it at 60, moving starts it, and it reaches 0 about one minute later.

  6. 06

    Send in the cops

    At 40 seconds left, one cop walks in from the front door and moves toward you, slower than the thief (about half the player speed). At 20 seconds left, a second cop does the same from the side door. They do not shoot. If one overlaps you, the round ends.

    Done when: overlapping a cop shows "Busted", and you can also reach the car before the first cop appears if you move as soon as the clock starts.

  7. 07

    Win, lose, and play again

    Touching the car ends the round: "Got away" at $5,000 or more, "Not enough" below that. If the clock hits 0 first, show "Too late". R restarts BankScene from a clean room, with the old cops gone. Put one line on screen that names the move keys and R.

    Done when: you can win once, lose by each of the three failures, press R, and play a second round without refreshing the page.
