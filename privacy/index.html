Privacy

StackPodium is run from Spain. For any privacy question or request, write to joanpau.ch22@gmail.com.

This website

  • There are no accounts and no forms on this site, and we set no cookies of our own.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub receives your IP address and may keep it in server logs for security and operation.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

The newsletter

  • You subscribe on a page run by beehiiv, our email provider, based in the United States. beehiiv stores your email address and records opens and clicks.
  • We use your address only to send the newsletter. We do not sell it or share it with sponsors.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your data.

Links to other sites

We link to the tools we rank. Those sites have their own privacy policies. If we add affiliate links, they will be labelled.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).
